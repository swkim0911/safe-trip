[Purpose]

Collect real first-hand scam cases that happened abroad.
Keep only texts with useful, experience-based information for other travelers.

[Evaluation Criteria]

1. Travel scam experience

  The text must clearly describe a real scam personally experienced by the author abroad, written in first-person perspective (e.g., "I", "we", "my experience").
  Examples: pickpocketing/theft, aggressive sales/solicitation, taxi/transport, lodging, restaurant/bar/cafe, financial/exchange, others.
  Exclude: General advice or safety tips without a personal incident, Poor service complaints or vague, generic grievances, News articles, rumors, or second-hand reports, Jokes, satire, or humorous exaggerations, Advertisements or promotional content, Experiences that happened to others (not first-hand)

2. Value for sharing

  Must mention a country, or a subnational region (such as a city, state, or province) where the corresponding country can reasonably be inferred.
  The text must provide enough context to be useful for other travelers. Specifically, it should mention:
    Where the scam happened
    How the scam was carried out
    What result or outcome occurred (e.g., money lost, forced payment, learned lesson)

  Exclude: texts that fail to provide at least two of the above three elements

[Decision Rules]

- If all criteria are met AND the text clearly covers where, how, and what result (at least two elements required) → 1
- If the scam is mentioned but the text lacks context → 0
- If any criterion is not met or it’s uncertain if the scam was personally experienced → 0
- Output must be a single digit 1 or 0 only. No other text, spaces, or explanations.
