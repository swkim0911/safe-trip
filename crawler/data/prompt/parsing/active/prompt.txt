[Action]
Select exactly ONE of the following values for the `action` field (string, case-sensitive):

- `Theft/Pickpocketing`
- `Overcharging`
- `Aggressive Solicitation`
- `Fraud`
- `System Tampering`
- `Other Action`

Guidelines:
- `Theft/Pickpocketing`: theft of personal belongings, either directly from the person (pockets, bags, backpacks) or from nearby unattended items (hotel room, luggage, cafe table, etc.), where the main intent is to steal money or valuables.
- `Overcharging`: the traveler agreed to a service or product, but was charged an unfair or inflated price (taxi fare manipulation, inflated restaurant bills, tour price disputes, shops charging tourists more). Use this even if the seller is aggressive when demanding payment, as long as the traveler initially agreed to use the service.
- `Aggressive Solicitation`: someone forced or pressured the traveler into accepting something they did NOT want or agree to, then demanded payment (forced bracelets, unwanted photos, unsolicited guides, street performers who demand money after performing without being asked). Use this when an unwanted item or service is pushed onto the traveler first.
- `Fraud`: deceptive schemes involving lies or fake identities/services (fake tours, fake tickets, fake police, fake officials, fake rental listings, etc.).
- `System Tampering`: manipulation of payment or service systems (card skimming, rigged ATMs, forced add-ons, meter tampering, unauthorized card charges).
- `Other Action`: use only if none of the above categories reasonably fit.

Use exact label strings from the list.

[Context]
Select exactly ONE of the following values (string, case-sensitive):

- `Transportation Service`
- `Accommodation Service`
- `Food & Beverage Service`
- `Financial Service`
- `Tourism Service`
- `Street Encounter`
- `Other`

Guidelines:
- `Transportation Service`: using taxis, rideshares, buses, trains, subways, shuttles, ferries, or any form of transportation. Includes fare disputes, meter tampering, and theft while on transit.
- `Accommodation Service`: booking or staying at hotels, hostels, guesthouses, Airbnbs, vacation rentals. Includes check-in/out issues, hidden charges, fake bookings.
- `Food & Beverage Service`: dining or drinking at restaurants, bars, cafes, nightclubs, pubs, street food vendors. Includes bill padding, overcharging, and forced consumption.
- `Financial Service`: using ATMs, currency exchange booths, banks, money transfer services, payment terminals. Includes card skimming, exchange rate scams, fake ATMs.
- `Tourism Service`: tours, guided services, attraction tickets, museum entries, theme parks, sightseeing activities. Includes fake tickets, overpriced tours, unauthorized guides.
- `Street Encounter`: unsolicited or informal interactions in public spaces such as streets, plazas, parks, markets, beaches. Includes bracelet scams, forced gifts, street sellers, pickpocketing, aggressive solicitation.
- `Other`: use only if none of the above categories reasonably fit.

Use exact label strings from the list.
