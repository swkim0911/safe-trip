[Purpose]

Collect real first-hand scam cases that happened abroad.
Keep only texts with useful, experience-based information for other travelers.

[Evaluation Criteria]

1. Personal first-hand experience
  The text must clearly describe a real scam personally experienced by the author abroad, written in first-person perspective (e.g., "I", "we", "my experience").
  Examples: pickpocketing, theft, aggressive sales, overcharging, fraud, system tampering
  Exclude: General advice or safety tips without a personal incident, poor service complaints or vague grievances, news articles, rumors, or second-hand reports, jokes, satire, or humorous exaggerations, advertisements or promotional content, experiences that happened to others (not first-hand)

2. Value for sharing
  Must mention a country, or a subnational region (such as a city, state, or province) where the corresponding country can reasonably be inferred.
  The text must provide enough context to be useful for other travelers. Specifically, it should mention at least TWO of the following:
    - Where the scam happened (specific location)
    - How the scam was carried out (method/tactics)
    - What result or outcome occurred (impact/consequence)
  
  Exclude: texts that provide fewer than two of the above elements

[Decision Rules]

Output "1" if ALL of the following are met:
- First-person travel scam experience abroad
- Mentions location (country/region identifiable)
- Provides at least 2 of 3 context elements (where/how/what)

Output "0" if ANY of the following apply:
- Not first-person experience (second-hand, news, rumors)
- No location mentioned or not identifiable
- Lacks sufficient context (fewer than 2 elements)
- General advice, complaints, jokes, or promotional content

Output format: Single digit only (1 or 0). No explanations.

[Examples]

Example 1:
Text: "I was in Rome near the Colosseum when three men surrounded me. One distracted me asking for directions while another pickpocketed my wallet. I lost €200 and my credit cards."
Output: 1
Reason: First-person ✓, Location (Rome, Colosseum) ✓, Where/How/What all present ✓

Example 2:
Text: "Be careful of taxi scams in Southeast Asia. They often overcharge tourists."
Output: 0
Reason: No first-person experience, too vague, general advice only

Example 3:
Text: "My friend told me about a scam in Bangkok where a tuk-tuk driver took her to a fake jewelry store."
Output: 0
Reason: Second-hand experience (happened to friend), not first-person

Example 4:
Text: "I got scammed in Paris. It was terrible and I lost money."
Output: 0
Reason: Lacks context - no details about how the scam happened or what exactly occurred

Example 5:
Text: "In Barcelona, someone tried to sell me fake tickets to Sagrada Familia. I almost bought them but noticed they looked suspicious and walked away."
Output: 1
Reason: First-person ✓, Location (Barcelona, Sagrada Familia) ✓, Where/How/What provided ✓

Example 6:
Text: "Watch out for pickpockets in tourist areas! I read about it happening a lot."
Output: 0
Reason: No personal experience, just general warning based on reading
